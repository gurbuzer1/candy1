How to Play

Swap adjacent candies to make matches of 3 or more!

4 in a row creates a Striped candy that clears a whole row or column!

L or T shape creates a Wrapped candy that explodes in a 3x3 area!

5 in a row creates a Color Bomb that removes all candies of one color!

Reach the target score before you run out of moves!
